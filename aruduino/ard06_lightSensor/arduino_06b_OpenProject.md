# Arduino 06b: Open Project Challenge
## Medina County Career Center — Applications of AI

---

### The Challenge

Build something new with your Arduino that combines **at least two** inputs or outputs working together.

You can use anything from your kit — sensors, LEDs, buttons, buzzers, resistors, the photoresistor, or any other component you haven't tried yet.

---

### Requirements

1. **Use at least two different components** that work together (not counting wires/resistors/breadboard). For example:
   - Photoresistor + buzzer (alarm that triggers in the dark)
   - Button + LED + photoresistor (light-activated switch with manual override)
   - Two different sensors providing readings at the same time
   - A sensor controlling an LED pattern

2. **Your code must include at least one `if` statement** that makes a decision based on sensor input.

3. **Write a short description** (3-5 sentences) at the top of your code as a comment explaining:
   - What your project does
   - What components you used
   - What problem it solves or what it demonstrates

4. **Demo it** — be ready to show it working.

---

### Ideas to Get You Started

These are just suggestions. You're welcome to come up with your own idea.

- **Smart night light:** Photoresistor detects darkness and turns on an LED automatically
- **Intruder alert:** Photoresistor or button triggers a buzzer + flashing LED
- **Temperature + light monitor:** Read from two sensors and display different LED patterns based on combined conditions
- **Reaction time game:** LED lights up at a random time, player presses button as fast as possible, result shown via LED blinks or serial output
- **Morse code sender:** Button input translates to LED blinks with proper timing
- **Light meter:** Photoresistor readings shown as a bar of LEDs (more light = more LEDs on)
