Voltage Divider Tip: The photoresistor and 10KΩ resistor work together. As light increases, the photoresistor's resistance drops, which pushes the voltage at A0 higher. The Arduino reads this as a number from 0 (dark) to ~900+ (bright flashlight). The 10KΩ resistor is necessary — without it, the circuit doesn't produce a changing voltage for the Arduino to read.
